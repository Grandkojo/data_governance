## 1. Individual Customer Analysis

### Customer A: Abena (Accra, Ghana)
*   **Legal Right to Deletion?**: **Yes**. Under Section 28 of Ghana's Data Protection Act (Act 843), a data subject has the right to request a data controller to erase or destroy personal data that the controller is no longer authorized to retain.
*   **ShopGhana's Obligations**: The company must erase the data unless a legal justification for retention exists (e.g., tax law).
*   **Data Retention/Justification**: Transactional records/invoices must be retained for at least 6 years to comply with Ghana Revenue Authority (GRA) tax audit requirements.
*   **Response Deadline**: Act 843 does not specify a rigid number of days like GDPR, but "without undue delay" is the standard practice (typically interpreted as **21-30 days**).

### Customer B: Lukas (Berlin, Germany)
*   **Legal Right to Deatils?**: **Yes**. Under GDPR Article 17 (Right to Erasure), Lukas can request deletion immediately.
*   **Exemptions**: Since the purchase was 2 weeks ago, ShopGhana must retain data necessary for the "performance of a contract" (e.g., handling potential warranty claims or returns) and for legal/tax compliance (records must be kept for 10 years under German commercial law/HGB).
*   **Action Steps**: 1. Verify identity. 2. Remove from marketing. 3. Delete non-essential profiling data. 4. Flag transactional data for "Legal Hold" only.
*   **Response Deadline**: **30 Days** (one month).
*   **Missed Deadline Consequences**: Administrative fines up to €20 million or 4% of global annual turnover (whichever is higher).

### Customer C: Maria (Los Angeles, California)
*   **Legal Right to Deletion?**: **Limited**. While CCPA/CPRA grants a right to delete, an active return dispute is a valid exemption to "complete a transaction" or "maintain for internal use" (Cal. Civ. Code § 1798.105(d)).
*   **ShopGhana's Response**: Deletion can be **deferred** until the dispute is resolved. However, the "Do Not Sell" request must be honored within 15 business days.
*   **"Stop Selling" Request**: ShopGhana must stop sharing Maria's data with 3rd-party advertisers/affiliates and notify them of the opting-out.
*   **Response Deadline**: **45 Days** for deletion, **15 Business Days** for "Do Not Sell".
*   **Disclosures**: Must inform Maria of the specific exemption used to deny/delay the deletion.
